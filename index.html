Our story

Built by Stella, for everyone who misses home

Two years of sourcing, processing and shipping the real taste of Nigeria with hygiene and honesty at the centre.

Kamto Foods started with a simple frustration: it was too hard for Nigerians abroad to get real, clean, trustworthy foodstuffs from home.

Stella grew up around markets she knew good egusi from filler, real Ijebu garri from the rest, and how much hygiene gets lost between the stall and the pot. So she built a business around doing it properly: sourcing directly, processing in a clean facility, and sealing everything for the long journey abroad.

Over 2+ years, Kamto Foods has grown from friends-and-family orders into a name the diaspora trusts shipping to the UK, USA, Canada and beyond, and running local market runs for busy people back home.
